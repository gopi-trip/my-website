This is a [digital garden](https://maggieappleton.com/garden-history): a
collection of notes that are published while they're still growing. Nothing
here is finished; some things are more finished than others.

## Growth stages

- **seedling** — a rough idea, a list of links, or a question. Probably wrong somewhere.
- **budding** — has a shape and some explanation. Still has gaps I know about.
- **evergreen** — I'd be happy for a stranger to learn from it.

## Linking

Notes link to each other with double brackets: `[[attention]]` becomes
[[attention]], and `[[kl-divergence|a custom label]]` becomes
[[kl-divergence|a custom label]]. At the bottom of every note is a list of
everything on the site that links back to it.

Folders become categories, so `wiki/ml/attention.md` lives under `ml/`.

## Rules

1. Write it in my own words.
2. Link it to at least one other note.
3. Be honest about the stage.
