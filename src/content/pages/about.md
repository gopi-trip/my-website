## the short version

I'm a developer interested in machine learning, the systems that run it, and
the strange gap between "it works" and "I understand why it works". I like
small tools, plain text, and code you can read in one sitting.

## what i spend my time on

- **ML, from the inside.** Training small models from scratch, reading their
  weights like tea leaves, writing down what I find in the [wiki](/wiki/).
- **Software that stays put.** Static sites, command-line tools, things that
  will still build in ten years.
- **Reading.** Mostly papers, some novels, and too many books about how other
  people think. See the [bookshelf](/bookshelf/).

## things i care about

- Explaining things simply without making them wrong.
- Writing down negative results.
- The small web: personal sites, RSS, pages that load before you blink.
- Leaving codebases (and campsites) a little better than I found them.

## about this site

Built by hand with [Astro](https://astro.build), Markdown, and a monospace
font. The little pixel icons are drawn character-by-character in a text file.
There's [a post about how it's made](/writings/building-this-site/). No
analytics, no cookies, no newsletter popups.
